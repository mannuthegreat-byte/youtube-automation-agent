## Capture notes

- The hook promises a reaction **before the cops arrive**, so only post a take where
  the driver does something visible and interesting first. If the driver just sits
  there, the clip breaks its own promise. Record several crashes and keep the best reaction.
- Don't add a caption that explains what happens. The curiosity gap is the whole clip.
- No end card, no "subscribe" text. It kills the loop.
